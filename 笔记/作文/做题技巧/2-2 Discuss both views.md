### 示范题目

Some people think online reading will replace printed books and newspapers in the future, while others believe printed media will always exist. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
有人认为未来网络阅读会取代纸质书报，另一些人则认为纸质媒介不会消失。讨论双方观点并给出自己看法。

---

==It is often debated that== traditional printed books and newspapers will be completely replaced by free online reading in the future.

==Opinions vary greatly on this issue. Some people believe that== digital reading will dominate people’s daily life, ==while others argue that== printed publications still have irreplaceable value.

==This essay will discuss both perspectives and present my own opinion.==

**翻译**：人们经常争论，传统纸质书籍和报纸未来会被免费的网络阅读彻底取代。关于这一问题人们观点不一，一部分人认为数字化阅读将主导人们的日常生活，而另一部分人主张纸质读物拥有不可替代的价值。本文将讨论双方观点并给出个人看法。

---

==On the one hand, those who support the former view hold the valid reason that== online reading resources are more convenient and cost-free for the public.

==Since== massive reading materials can be accessed anytime and anywhere on the Internet, ==it is beneficial for== ordinary people ==to== gain knowledge with zero cost.

==This can greatly help== citizens ==to improve their ability in== absorbing new information efficiently.

==A typical example is that== numerous news articles and e-books are available online for free, which has greatly reduced people’s reliance on physical books.

**翻译**：一方面，支持前者观点的人群有合理理由认为，网络阅读资源对大众而言更加便捷且免费。由于人们可以随时随地在网络上获取海量阅读资料，这有利于民众零成本获取知识。这能极大帮助人们高效吸收新信息。典型的例子是，大量新闻与电子书可在线免费阅览，大幅降低了人们对实体书籍的依赖。

---

==On the other hand, opponents of this view claim that== printed books and newspapers possess unique reading experience and collection value.

==As== screen reading may cause eye strain and distraction, ==it may lead to negative consequences for== people’s reading quality and concentration.

==Rather than bringing benefits, this trend may cause problems such as== superficial reading and information fragmentation.

==For instance,== most online readers tend to skim quickly instead of deep thinking, while printed books encourage people to focus and study carefully.

**翻译**：另一方面，反对该观点的人认为，纸质书籍和报纸拥有独特的阅读体验与收藏价值。由于屏幕阅读容易造成视疲劳、使人分心，这会对人们的阅读质量和专注力带来负面影响。该趋势不仅无法带来益处，还会造成浅层阅读、信息碎片化等问题。举例来说，大多数网络读者习惯快速浏览、缺乏深度思考，而纸质书籍更能让人专注精读。

---

==Overall, both sides have their own merits.==

==From my perspective, I am more convinced that== printed media will never be fully replaced by online reading ==is more reasonable.==

==This is because it can not only== provide a more immersive reading experience, ==but also== preserve cultural inheritance and reading authenticity ==in the long term.==

**翻译**：总体而言，双方观点都有合理之处。在我看来，我更认同纸质媒介不会被网络阅读彻底取代这一观点。这是因为从长远来看，纸质读物不仅能提供沉浸式阅读体验，还能更好地传承文化、保证阅读的真实性。
