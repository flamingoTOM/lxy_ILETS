### 例题

Access to clean water is a basic human right. Therefore every home should have a water supply that is provided free of charge. Do you agree or disagree?
获得洁净水源是一项基本人权。因此，每户家庭都应当享有免费供水。你是否同意这一观点？

---

==It is sometimes argued that== access to clean water is a fundamental human right, so all households ought to receive free tap water. 
==Personally, I largely disagree with this view, as== providing free water supply ==brings more drawbacks than benefits== for society.

**翻译**：有些人认为，获取洁净水源是一项基本人权，因此所有家庭都应当享有免费自来水供应。就我个人而言，我大体不赞同这一观点，因为推行免费供水对社会而言弊大于利。

---

==First and foremost, one strong argument in favor of== charging residents for water ==is that== water resources on Earth are limited. 
==Since== unlimited free water may encourage wasteful habits, ==it can contribute to== the overconsumption of fresh water. 
==This is particularly true for people== living in water-rich regions, ==as== they may ignore the importance of water conservation without proper restrictions. 
==For example,== many families would use large volumes of clean water for watering gardens or washing vehicles if they do not need to pay the bill.

**翻译**：首先，支持向居民收取水费的一个有力论据是，地球上的水资源是有限的。由于无限制的免费供水会助长浪费习惯，这会加剧淡水资源过度消耗。这一点对于水资源丰富地区的居民尤为适用，因为缺少约束时，他们容易忽视节水的重要性。例如，如果无需缴纳水费，许多家庭会大量使用洁净水源浇灌花园、清洗车辆。

---

==In addition, another strong justification for== paid water supply is that running water systems require continuous investment. 
==Because== water pipelines, purification facilities and maintenance teams need financial support, charging fees enables local governments to keep the whole water supply system operating well. 
==Instead of== relying completely on public tax revenue, water fees can cover daily operational costs. 
==For instance==, cities can use collected water charges to upgrade old pipelines and reduce water leakage.

**翻译**：此外，支持收费供水的另一个充分理由是，供水系统需要持续投入资金。由于输水管道、净水设施和维护团队都需要资金支撑，收取水费能够让地方政府保障整套供水系统平稳运转。相比于完全依靠税收，水费可以承担日常运营开销。举例来说，城市能够利用水费收入改造老旧管道，减少水资源渗漏。

---

==However, it is important to note the negative side of== water pricing. ==Although this approach may be beneficial to resource protection in many ways, it can also be harmful to== low-income groups. 
==Specifically,== impoverished families may struggle to afford water bills when prices keep rising. 
==One case in point is that== some disadvantaged households in remote areas have to cut down daily water use to reduce living expenses.

**翻译**：然而，我们需要留意收费供水存在的弊端。尽管这种做法在很多方面有利于资源保护，它同样会对低收入群体造成不利影响。具体而言，物价持续上涨时，贫困家庭可能难以承担水费。一个恰当的例子是，偏远地区部分困难家庭不得不削减日常用水量，以此降低生活开支。

---

==In conclusion, although== charging for tap water ==may occasionally bring minor downsides such as== financial pressure on poor citizens, 
==I still believe that== water supply should not be completely free for all families, as it can prevent serious water waste and maintain public water infrastructure in the long run.

**翻译**：总而言之，尽管自来水收费偶尔会带来一些小弊端，例如给贫困人群造成经济压力，我依旧认为不应当向所有家庭免费供水，因为从长远来看，该举措能够避免严重的水资源浪费，并且维持公共供水设施正常运转。
