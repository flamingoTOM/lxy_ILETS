

## 随着...的发展
| 中文           | 英文表达                               | 难度      | 备注 & 适用场景                |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------ |
| 随着社会的发展 / 进步 | With the development of society    | 6.0 基础款 | 万能通用，最容易背诵               |
|              | With the ==progress of society==   | 6.5     | progress 侧重 “进步”，人文类话题更佳 |
| 随着科技的发展 / 进步 | With the development of technology | 6.0 基础  | 通用写法                     |
| 随着经济全球化      | With ==economic globalisation==    | 6.5 首选  | **重点！名词短语，直接套用**         |


## 阻止，阻碍
| 英文表达         | 词性  | 中文释义           | 写作例句                                                                           |
| ------------ | --- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **barrier**  | n.  | 障碍，阻碍（⭐首选，最高频） | Language can be a barrier to international communication.<br><br>语言会成为国际交流的阻碍。 |
| **impede**   | v.  | 阻碍，妨碍（高分动词）    | High costs impede the popularity of new technology.<br><br>高昂成本阻碍新技术普及。        |
| **restrict** | v.  | 限制，阻碍发展        | Financial pressure restricts people’s choices.<br><br>经济压力阻碍人们的选择。             |
| **prevent**  | v.  | 阻止，阻碍（基础简单词）   | Such problems prevent society from developing rapidly.                         |

## 忽视

| 英语表达          | 中文释义    | 适用语境              | 例句（雅思写作风格）                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **overlook**  | 忽略、未注意到 | 指无意中漏掉或未充分重视      | _The government tends to **overlook** the needs of rural communities._                     |
| **neglect**   | 疏忽、疏于照顾 | 指未尽到责任或义务（常带负面评价） | _Parents who **neglect** their children's emotional development may cause long-term harm._ |
| **disregard** | 漠视、不理会  | 指完全不把某事物当回事（语气很强） | _A complete **disregard** for environmental regulations will invite severe penalties._     |

### 居民，人，劳动力

| 表达                  | 释义                  | 例句                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| ==residents==       | 居民（首选，楼房、社区、城市）     | Local residents have little access to sunlight.   |
| inhabitants         | 居民、常住者（偏书面，城市 / 地区） | The inhabitants suffer from blocked sunlight.     |
| ==individuals==     | 人们，个体（写作高频）         | 正式，议论文万能替换 people                                 |
| citizens            | 市民（城市、公共政策话题）       | Citizens hope for better living environments.     |
| ==the public==      | 大众，公众（公共设施、安全政策）    | The public need effective safety measures.        |
| workers / employees | 上班族（职场话题）           | New employees are less capable than senior staff. |
| ==workforce==       | 全体从业人员、劳动力【强烈推荐】    | The company’s workforce needs emergency training. |

### 实施，出台，颁布，推行
| 英文词汇          | 中文对应      | 例句                                               |
| ------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| introduce     | 出台（政策）    | The government introduced new housing policies.  |
| ==launch==    | 推行、推出（计划） | The city launched a plan to build more flats.    |
| ==implement== | 实施（落实执行）  | The new rules were implemented last month.       |
| carry out     | 实施、落实     | Local councils carry out the housing policy.     |
| enforce       | 强制执行      | Strict building laws are enforced in big cities. |


### 处理，应对
| 英文表达          | 中文含义         | 使用场景 & 区别               | 例句                                                   |
| ------------- | ------------ | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| ==cope with== | 应对（难题、压力）    | 侧重主动面对困难、压力、社会问题        | Many young people can’t cope with high living costs. |
| ==deal with== | 处理，应对        | 万能基础词，人 / 事 / 问题通用，口语首选 | Governments need to deal with housing shortages.     |
| handle        | 妥善处理、应付      | 侧重有能力处理事务、麻烦            | Cities need to handle the fast growth of population. |
| resolve       | 解决（纠纷、矛盾、问题） | 侧重彻底化解问题                | These rules help resolve the lack of homes.          |

### 严重的，恶劣的
| 单词       | 侧重点                 | 例句                                                   |
| -------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| serious  | 通用，可形容问题、情况、后果      | Serious housing shortages appear in big cities.      |
| severe   | 程度极强，恶劣严峻（压力、短缺、天气） | Young people face severe pressure from living costs. |
| critical | 情况危急、十分严重           | The lack of affordable houses has become critical.   |


### 减轻（压力，负担）
| 英文      | 搭配 & 用法                                   | 例句                                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| reduce  | reduce pressure /reduce burdens 通用        | The policy can reduce people’s living pressure.   |
| ease    | ease pressure /==ease the burden==（高频）    | Building cheap flats eases young people’s burden. |
| relieve | relieve stress / relieve financial burden | More affordable housing relieves economic stress. |
