### 例题

Nowadays, many young people choose to study abroad. Is it positive or negative?
现如今，许多年轻人选择出国留学。这一现象是积极的还是消极的？

---

==It is widely observed that== an increasing number of young people choose to pursue further education in foreign countries.
==From my perspective, this trend brings more advantages than disadvantages== for young students.

**翻译**：如今人们普遍注意到，越来越多的年轻人选择出国深造。在我看来，这一趋势对青年学生而言利大于弊。

---

==First and foremost, the most obvious advantage of this phenomenon is that== studying abroad helps students improve their academic ability and career competitiveness.

==Since== top overseas universities have advanced teaching resources and research platforms, ==it can contribute to== students’ professional development in their fields.

==This is particularly true for students== who major in science and engineering, ==as== they can gain access to cutting-edge technology and world-class scholars.

==For example,== many Chinese engineering students return with stronger research skills after studying abroad, which makes them popular among top companies.

**翻译**：首先，这一现象最显著的好处是，出国留学有助于学生提升学术能力与就业竞争力。由于海外顶尖高校拥有先进的教学资源与科研平台，这能够助力学生在自身领域获得专业成长。这一点对于理工科学生尤为适用，因为他们可以接触前沿技术与世界级学者。例如，很多中国工科留学生毕业后科研能力显著提升，深受头部企业青睐。

---

==In addition, another positive effect of this trend is that== overseas study cultivates students’ independence and broadens their horizons.

==Because== students have to handle daily life and academic problems alone in a foreign country, ==it enables them to== develop better problem-solving and cross-cultural communication skills.

==Instead of== staying in a familiar environment and relying on their parents, young people can experience diverse cultures and lifestyles.

==For instance,== students living abroad often learn to manage their budget, arrange schedules and deal with cultural differences independently.

**翻译**：此外，这一趋势的另一项积极影响是，留学会培养学生的独立性、拓宽其视野。由于学生在异国必须独自处理生活与学业问题，这能够锻炼他们解决问题与跨文化沟通的能力。相比于待在熟悉的环境里依赖父母，年轻人可以体验多元的文化与生活方式。举例来说，在海外生活的学生通常能学会独立管理开支、规划时间、应对文化差异。

---

==However, we cannot ignore the negative side of this trend.==

==Although this phenomenon may be beneficial to== students’ long-term development ==in many ways,==

==it can also bring negative effects to== their mental state and family finance.

==Specifically,== many young students suffer from loneliness and cultural shock when they first arrive in a foreign country.

==One case in point is that== some students cannot adapt to the new teaching mode and living environment, which leads to anxiety and poor academic performance.

**翻译**：然而，我们不能忽视这一趋势的消极面。尽管该现象在很多方面有利于学生的长远发展，它也会给学生的心理状态与家庭经济带来负面影响。具体而言，很多年轻学生初到异国时会感到孤独、遭遇文化冲击。一个恰当的例子是，部分学生无法适应全新的教学模式与生活环境，进而产生焦虑情绪、学业表现下滑。

---

==In conclusion, although this trend may occasionally bring minor downsides such as== cultural adaptation pressure and financial burden,

==I still believe that it is an overall positive development, as it can== greatly promote young people’s academic progress and personal growth.

**翻译**：总而言之，尽管出国留学偶尔会带来文化适应压力、经济负担等小弊端，我依旧认为这整体是积极的发展趋势，因为它能极大推动年轻人的学业进步与个人成长。
