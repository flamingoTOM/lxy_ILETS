To what extent ==do you agree or disagree with this statement==?

It is sometimes argued that building tall apartment blocks is the best way to provide enough homes in big cities. Pesonally, I largely disagree with it, as it brings more downsides than benefits.

First and foremost, a strong argument in opposite of this opinion is that the ability to prevent risks for tall apartments is less capable than noraml buildings. Because of the high altitude, more safety precautions should be done and managers need to inspect buildings in the long term in order to against emergency such as fire. For instance, When strong wind like typhoon is coming, people who live in high storey will be more dangerous, especially fo those who live in tall apartments built along the coastline. 

In addition, another strong justification for this view is that living in tall apartment blocks is less confortable and convenient. Since high buildings block sunlight, residents who walk or relax on the ground will lack sufficient sunlight. Apart from this, inhabitants who live in tall apartment blocks will cost more time to wait for elevators, which makes their commuting time grow sharply. 

However, it is important to note the positive side of this opinion. Although this may be negative to society in many ways, it can also be beneficial for large cities to provide enough homes. This is because tall apartments can hold more residents per unit area, which enables more people to live in the city center. It is particular true for those developing countries and cities. One case in point is that many countries lead to build more tall apartment blocks for attracting labour force. Both Shang Hai and Bei Jing was that in the history. 

In conclusion, although tall apartments may occasionally bring minor advantages such as providing enough homes for citizens. I still believe that the living quality and safety are more crucial for public.

1. **Pesonally** ❌拼写错误 → Personally
2. **in opposite of** ❌固定搭配错误
    ✅ in opposition to
3. **the ability … is less capable than normal buildings**【超级典型中式病句，致命】
    ❌核心问题：capable 主语必须是人 / 机构，**ability 不能用 be capable**；同时比较对象混乱（ability 和楼房对比）
    原意：高层建筑防范风险的能力弱于普通楼房
    ✅ Buildings are less capable of guarding against risks than low-rise buildings
4. **noraml** ❌拼写 → normal
5. **in order to against** ❌重大语法错误
    to 后面必须接动词原形；against 是介词
    ✅ in order to guard against
6. **emergency such as fire** ❌单复数混乱
    ✅ emergencies such as fires
7. **When** ❌句中非首词无需大写 → when
8. **strong wind like typhoon** ❌可数名词缺少冠词、单复数问题
    ✅ strong winds such as typhoons
9. **live in high storey** ❌地道表达错误
    ✅ live on high floors
10. **people will be more dangerous** ❌中式逻辑
    ❌人本身不是危险；是人面临危险
    ✅ residents face greater risks
11. **fo** ❌拼写 → for
12. **confortable** ❌拼写 → comfortable
13. **inhabitants… will cost more time** ❌主语搭配错误
    cost 的主语只能是事物，**人不能 cost 时间**
    ✅ residents spend more time waiting for lifts
14. **It is particular true** ❌词性错误，形容词不能修饰 true
    ✅ It is particularly true
15. **lead to build** ❌搭配错误
    lead to + doing；且语义不通。你想表达 “倡导、推动建设”
    ✅ advocate constructing /promote the construction of
16. **Shang Hai、Bei Jing** ❌英文城市标准写法
    ✅ Shanghai, Beijing（无空格）
17. **Both … was** ❌主谓一致错误
    两者作主语用复数 ✅ were
18. **was that in the history** ❌表达冗余、中式英语
    ✅ followed this pattern in history
19. **although … citizens.** ❌逗号拼接错误！
    although 引导的是状语从句，**不能独立成句**，此处句号属于严重断句错误
    ✅ Although … citizens, I still believe…
20. **for public** ❌缺少冠词
    ✅ for the public

## 第一段

- 原文：It is sometimes argued that building tall apartment blocks is the best way to provide ==enough homes== in big cities.
    
    修改：It is sometimes argued that building tall apartment blocks is the best way to provide ==enough **housing**== in big cities.
- 原文：==Pesonally==, I largely disagree with it, as it brings more downsides than benefits.
    
    修改：==**Personally**,== I largely disagree with **this view**, as it **creates** more drawbacks than benefits.

错误修正：

1. **Pesonally → Personally（拼写致命错误）**
2. enough homes → enough housing（雅思住房话题固定搭配）
3. it → this view，避免代词指代模糊（扣分点）
4. brings → creates；downsides=drawbacks，学术写作更合适

## 第二段（论点 1：高层安全隐患）

- 原文：First and foremost, a strong argument ==in opposite of this opinion== is that ==the ability to prevent risks== for tall apartments is less capable than ==noraml buildings.==
    
    修改：First and foremost, a strong argument ==**against** this opinion== is that high-rise apartments are more ==difficult to protect against potential risks== than ==**low-rise** buildings.==
- 原文：Because of the ==high altitude==, more ==safety precautions== ==should be done== and managers need to inspect buildings in the long term in order ==to against emergency such as fire.==
    
    修改：Due to their ==**height**==, ==stricter safety measures== ==must be implemented==, and managers need to carry out regular long-term inspections ==**to cope with emergencies** such as **fires**.==
- 原文：For instance, ==When strong wind like typhoon is coming==, people who live ==in high storey== ==will be more dangerous==, especially fo those who live in tall apartments built along the coastline.
    
    修改：For instance, ==**when severe winds or typhoons arrive**==, residents living ==on **upper floors**== ==face greater risks==, particularly those in tall apartment blocks built along coastlines.

❗致命错误清单：

1. in opposite of → **against（固定搭配错误，严重扣分）**
2. noraml → normal（拼写）
3. altitude 形容楼房属于用词错误！altitude = 海拔；楼房高度直接用 height
4. in order to against → **to 后面必须动词原形，against 是介词！重大语法错误**
5. emergency 可数 → emergencies；fire→fires
6. When 句中无需大写首字母
7. high storey → upper floors（地道表达）
8. people will be more dangerous 逻辑错误：**人不会危险，人面临危险** → face greater risks（中式英语高频扣分点）
9. fo → for 拼写

## 第三段（论点 2：舒适度低、通勤不便）

- 原文：In addition, another strong justification for this view is that living in tall apartment blocks is less ==confortable== and convenient.
    
    修改：In addition, another convincing reason is that living in tall apartment blocks is less ==**comfortable**== and convenient.
- 原文：Since high buildings block sunlight, residents who walk or relax on the ground will ==lack sufficient sunlight.==
    
    修改：High buildings block sunlight, meaning residents who walk or relax on the ground ==have limited access to natural light.==
- 原文：Apart from this, inhabitants who live in tall apartment blocks will ==cost more time== to wait for elevators, which makes their ==commuting time== ==grow sharply.==
    
    修改：Besides, inhabitants of tall apartment blocks often ==**waste considerable time**== waiting for elevators, which ==**significantly increases**== their ==daily travel time.==

❗致命错误：

1. confortable → comfortable（拼写）
2. people cost time **语法错误！人不能 cost time；事物占用时间，人 waste/spend time**（极高频语法错误）
3. grow sharply 搭配不当

## 第四段（让步段：高层优点）

- 原文：However, it is important to note the positive side of this opinion.
    
    修改：However, it is important to recognise the merits of this idea.
- 原文：Although this may be negative to society in many ways, it can also be beneficial for large cities to ==provide enough homes.==
    
    修改：Despite its various disadvantages, constructing tall apartment blocks helps large cities ==ease housing shortages.==
- 原文：This is because tall apartments can ==hold more residents== ==per unit areas==, which enables more people to live in the ==city center.==
    
    修改：This is because tall apartments can ==**accommodate** more residents== ==per unit area==, which enables more people to live in ==**city centres**.==
- 原文：It is particular true for those developing countries and cities.
    
    修改：This is **especially** true for cities in developing countries.
- 原文：One case in point is that many countries lead to build more tall apartment blocks ==for attracting labour force.==
    
    修改：One case in point is that many cities construct tall apartment blocks ==to attract **labour workers**.==
- 原文：Both ==Shang Hai and Bei Jing== ==was that== ==in the history.==
    
    修改：Both ==**Shanghai** and **Beijing== ==adopted this approach**== ==in their urban development history.==

❗致命错误：

1. particular → especially（词性错误！形容词不能修饰 true）
2. lead to build → 搭配错误，lead to doing；此处语义完全不通，直接改写
3. Shang Hai / Bei Jing → **Shanghai, Beijing（标准英文地名，不能空格）**
4. Both ... was → **主谓不一致！复数主语用 were**
5. in the history → in history /in their development history（多余 the）
6. labour force 前面缺少冠词

## 结尾段

- 原文：In conclusion, although tall apartments may occasionally bring minor advantages such as ==providing enough homes for citizens.==
    
    修改：In conclusion, even though tall apartments may offer certain benefits such as ==supplying housing for city residents==**,**
- 原文：I still believe that the ==living quality and safety== are more crucial ==for public.==
    
    修改：I still believe that ==**living standards** and public safety== are more crucial ==for **the public**.==

❗致命错误：

1. 【超级严重】although 引导从句，**不能单独成句！原句出现句号割裂复合句（句法错误，直接压分到 6 分以下）**
2. for public → for the public（缺少定冠词）
3. living quality → living standards（雅思固定表达）
