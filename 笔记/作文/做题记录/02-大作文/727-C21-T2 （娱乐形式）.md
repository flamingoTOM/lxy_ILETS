# 🔴 全部致命错误

## 一、拼写错误（硬性扣分，LR 大量减分）

1. bilibili → Bilibili；youtube → YouTube（专有名词必须大写）
2. ponds → pounds（英镑）
3. documentories → documentaries（纪录片）
4. communacte → communicate
5. coninced → convinced

## 二、语法致命错误（GRA 核心扣分点）

1. **残缺句（独立 Because/Since 分句，没有主句，雅思严重扣分）**
> Because social media…society. ❌
> Since these art forms bringing…vivid. ❌
> 语法规则：because/since 引导的从句**不能单独成句**。
2. **主谓不一致**
    platforms such as … **is** becoming → are becoming
3. **情态动词后动词原形硬性规则**
    could only takes → could only take
4. **非谓语错误**
    art forms bringing → art forms bring
    since 后面需要完整主谓，不能直接 doing
5. **not only…but also 平行结构错误**
    can not only provide … but also showing → but also show
6. **代词指代混乱**
    This is because **it** can not only…
    前文 theatres and cinemas 复数 → they
7. **冠词错误**
    a opportunity → an opportunity（元音开头）
    half day → half a day（缺少不定冠词）
8. **名词可数性错误**
    histories → history（历史，泛指概念不可数）
    cultures 此处泛指文化 → culture（不可数）

## 三、搭配错误（中式英语，考官一眼识别，LR+TA 扣分）
1. watch entertainment whatever they want
    语序问题：watch **whatever entertainment they want**
2. such as mobile phones and laptops
    逻辑错误：手机电脑**不是娱乐内容**，是观看工具，表达逻辑断裂
3. they cost almost nothing
    代词歧义：they 指代 people，语义变成 “人们几乎不花钱”，极易误解；本意是线上娱乐资源免费。
4. ❌ **watching theatres and cinemas** 超级严重中式英语
    不能 “观看剧院 / 电影院”
    正确：watch performances /films **in theatres and cinemas**
5. help young people to stimulate their interest
    搭配别扭：stimulate 兴趣主体是人，标准：spark/stimulate young people’s interest
6. every audience
    audience 集合名词，不能用 every 修饰
    改为：audience members /audiences
7. learn knowledge and cultures deeply
    中式搭配：learn knowledge 极少在学术写作使用；deeply 位置不当
8. talk with people in the history
    完全中式直译，无此表达，语义不通

## 四、逻辑与表达瑕疵（TA/CC）

1. “watch entertainment online” 表达笼统，entertainment 偏抽象，建议 entertainment content
2. vivid 形容现场氛围不合适；你积累的 immersive 更贴合影院 / 剧场场景
