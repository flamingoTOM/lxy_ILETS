It is widely observed that consumers can go to a supermarket and buy food produced all over the world. In my perspective, this trend brings more advantages than disadvantages.
First and foremost, the most obvious advantage of this phenomenon is that individuals will access more nutrients. With the development of the world trading, there are all kinds of food from different countries in the supermarket. The food basically contains more nutrients than those produced in local area. For example, in today's society, consumers can buy a bottle of milk produced in Austrilia, which contains more protein and vitamin D.
In addition, another positive effect of this trend is that it can promote the economic development all over the world. This is because food interaction between different countries or areas can really increase incomes of companies and employees, it contribute to more consumption. This is particularly true for agricultural countries. One case in point is that the export of cherries has bocome the main reason of the economic growth in Chilly.
However, we cannot neglect the negative side of this phenomenon. Although this trend may be beneficial for countries and individuals in many ways, it can also bring negative side such as slowing down local economic development. It is no doubt that consumers prefer to choose high-quality food with low price, it may be harmful to some food produced in local area.
In conclusion, although this trend may occasionally bring minor disadvantage such as impeding local economic growth, I still believe that it is an overall positive development for countries and individuals.

> **原句 1**: It is widely observed that consumers can go to a supermarket and buy food produced all over the world. In my perspective, this trend brings more advantages than disadvantages.  
> **修改 1**: **It is true that** consumers can go to a supermarket and buy food **from** all over the world. In my perspective, this trend brings **more benefits than drawbacks**.  
> _(理由：advantages/disadvantages与题目中的positive/negative不完全对等，改用benefits/drawbacks更准确；from替代produced避免重复；is true替代is widely observed更自然。)_

---

> **原句 2**: First and foremost, the most obvious advantage of this phenomenon is that ==individuals will access more nutrients.==  
> **修改 2**: **First of all**, the most obvious advantage of this phenomenon is that individuals ==**can access** more **types of** nutrients.==  
> _(理由：will access时态不当，客观事实用can access；补充types of使逻辑完整：不是“更多营养”而是“更多种类的营养”。)_

---

> **原句 3**: ==With the development of== ==the world trading==, ==there are all kinds of food== from different countries ==in the supermarket.==  
> **修改 3**: ==**Thanks to** the development of== ==**global trade**==, ==**a wide variety of** food== from different countries ==**is available** in supermarkets.==  
> _(理由：world trading搭配不当→global trade；there are...in the supermarket主语过重，改为is available更简洁。)_

---

> **原句 4**: The ==food== basically contains more nutrients ==than those produced in local area.==  
> **修改 4**: **Some ==imported food**== contains **different** nutrients ==**from** those **found in** locally **produced** food.==  
> _(理由：原句说“基本含有更多营养”不够严谨且缺少论证；改为“不同营养”避免武断；those produced in local area改为locally produced food更简洁。)_

---

> **原句 5**: For example, in today's society, consumers can buy a bottle of milk ==produced in Austrilia==, which ==contains== more protein and vitamin D.  
> **修改 5**: For example, consumers can buy a bottle of milk ==**from Australia**==, which ==**is said to contain**== more protein and vitamin D.  
> _(理由：修正拼写Austrilia→Australia；in today's society冗余删除；which contains改为is said to contain使论证更谨慎客观。)_

---

> **原句 6**: In addition, another positive effect of this trend is that it can promote ==the economic development all over the world.==  
> **修改 6**: **Moreover,** another positive effect of this trend is that it can promote ==**global** economic development.==  
> _(理由：In addition和another语义重复，换为Moreover；all over the world简化为global。)_

---

> **原句 7**: This is because ==food interaction== between different countries or areas ==can really increase== incomes of companies and employees, ==it contribute to== more consumption.  
> **修改 7**: This is because ==**food trade**== between different countries ==**increases**== the incomes of companies and employees, ==**which contributes** to== more consumption.  
> _(理由：food interaction搭配不当→food trade；修正主谓不一致contribute→contributes；修正run-on句，用which连接。)_

---

> **原句 8**: This is particularly true for agricultural countries. One case in point is that the export of cherries has ==bocome== ==the main reason== of the economic growth in ==Chilly.==  
> **修改 8**: This is particularly true for **agricultural** countries. One case in point is that the export of cherries has ==**become**== ==**a major driver**== of economic growth in ==**Chile**.==  
> _(理由：修正拼写bocome→become，Chilly→Chile；the main reason of改为a major driver of更地道。)_

---

> **原句 9**: However, we cannot neglect the negative side of this phenomenon.  
> **修改 9**: However, **this trend also has a downside**.  
> _(理由：we cannot neglect过于口语化；negative side of this phenomenon重复且冗余，简化为has a downside。)_

---

> **原句 10**: Although this trend may be beneficial for countries and individuals in many ways, it can also ==bring negative side== such as ==slowing down local economic development.==  
> **修改 10**: Although this trend **is** beneficial **in** many ways, it can also ==**have a negative impact== ==on local economies**.==  
> _(理由：may be过于模糊，直接用is；for...in many ways改为in many ways更简洁；bring negative side such as slowing down改为have a negative impact on更简洁。)_

---

> **原句 11**: ==It is no doubt that== consumers prefer to choose high-quality food ==with low price,== it may be harmful to some ==food produced in local area.==  
> **修改 11**: ==**There is no doubt that**== consumers prefer to choose high-quality food ==**at a** low price==, **which** may be harmful to ==**locally produced food**.==  
> _(理由：修正run-on句，用which连接；修正冠词with low price→at a low price；some food produced in local area→locally produced food。)_

---

> **原句 12**: In conclusion, although this trend may occasionally bring minor disadvantage such as impeding local economic growth, I still believe that it is an overall positive development for countries and individuals.  
> **修改 12**: In conclusion, although this trend **has** occasional **drawbacks** such as **harming** local **economies**, I still believe that the **benefits far outweigh the disadvantages**.  
> _(理由：bring minor disadvantage→has occasional drawbacks；impeding→harming；overall positive development→benefits far outweigh the disadvantages，与首段呼应。)_
