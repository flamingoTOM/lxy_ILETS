Access to clean water is a basic human right. Therefore every home should have a water supply that is provided free of charge. ==Do you agree or disagree?==




It is sometimes argued that every home should be provided water for free, since accessing clean water is a basic human right. Personally, I largely disagree with this opinion, as there are more downsides than benefits.

First and foremost, a strong argument against this view is that charging for water can help people develop a good habit of saving it. Since the existence of this rule, people will have lower chance to forget shutting down taps in their house. 

In addition, another strong justification for this opinion is that some people may use their free water to make some illegal profits. Because the water in some factories and markets isn't for free, as some people may transfer water from thier house to these places in secret. For instance, if a person who lives in the first floor could transfer his free water to some adjacent shops to make profits illegally.

However, it's vital to note the positive side of this view. Although this approach may be harmful to society in many ways, it can also be beneficial to some poor families. Because if the water fee is paid by the government or country, it will be a huge relief for these families. One case in point is that after the targeted subsidies about supplying free water to people in real poverty which was launched by Chinese government, the investigation showed that poor family could eat more food and wear warmer than before.

In conclusion, although free water for every home may occasionally bring minor advantages, I still believe that paying for water is crucial for everyone to keep a good habit and obey the laws.
