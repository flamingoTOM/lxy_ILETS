## 段落 3 饼图主体段

> To begin with, the percentage of full-time undergraduate accounts for 44%, which ranks the first in library users.

10. 名词单复数：full-time undergraduates
11. 冗余语病：the percentage … accounts for ❌
    
    ✅优化：Full-time undergraduates account for 44% of library users.

> In contrast, academic staff and distance learning of all courses make up the less proporation, at 7% and 8% respectively.

12. 拼写：proportion
13. 比较级误用：the less proportion ❌
    
    ✅the smallest proportions

> Besides, the percentage of full-time postgraduate make up for a quarter, while the proporation of part-time postgraduate constitute 16%.

14. ✅致命主谓一致：percentage … **makes**；proportion … **constitutes**
15. 多余介词：make up for ❌（make up = 占比；make up for 是 “弥补” 含义）
16. 单复数：postgraduates
17. 逻辑混乱：你先说 full-time 研究生（25% a quarter），句子语序写得极易混淆读者

## 段落 4 表格主体段（错误最多！）

> Apart from this, all readers satisfied with the helpfulness of staff and library opening hours, while 65% of them very satisfied with the former and 95% of them very satisfied with the latter.

18. ❌致命缺失谓语！
    
    `all readers satisfied` / `65% of them very satisfied` 没有 be 动词
    
    ✅ all readers **are satisfied**；65% of them **are** very satisfied
19. ❌【最高等级致命错误：信息颠倒】
    
    former=staff helpfulness；latter=opening hours
    
    你数据写反：
    
    原题：
    
    Opening hours:65% very satisfied
    
    Staff help:95% very satisfied
    
    你句子写：65%（staff），95%（opening hours），事实写错，Task Achievement 大幅扣分！

> A samll part of readers present that they are not satisfied with the availability of books, availability of journals and reliability of wi-fi, at 10%, 20% and 19% respectively.

20. 拼写：samll → small
21. 用词不当：present ❌ 这里不适合；✅state /report
22. 小瑕疵：Wi-Fi 需要大写

> In addition, nearly half of users show that they are very satisfied with them, at 50%, 45% and 48% respectively.

23. “them” 指代模糊，考官分不清指代什么，扣分

> The rest of the readers are farily satisfied with them.

24. 拼写：farily → **fairly**

## 开头段

原句：The pie chart and table illustrate the outcome of ==a investigation== of library users at a university.

修改：The pie chart and table illustrate the results of ==**an** investigation== of library users at a university.

改动：a → **an**（investigation 元音开头；outcome 替换成 results，贴合题目 survey 语境）

## Overview 总结段

原句：Overall, nearly half of library users is full-time undergraduate, which makes up ==the most proporation.==

修改：Overall, nearly half of library users **are** full-time **undergraduates**, which makes up ==**the largest proportion**.==

改动：is → **are**（主谓一致）；undergraduate → **undergraduates**（一类人群用复数）；most proporation → **largest proportion**（固定表达 + 修正拼写）

原句：Apart from this, ==the satisfaction in== helpfulness of staff ranks the first in this survey, ==all investigators satisfied with it.==

修改：Apart from this, ==satisfaction **with**== the helpfulness of staff ==received the highest ratings== in this survey, **and ==all respondents were satisfied with this service**.==

改动：satisfaction in → **satisfaction with**（固定搭配）；修复碎片句（补充谓语）；investigators → **respondents**（图表标准用词）

## 饼图主体段

原句：To begin with, the percentage of full-time undergraduate accounts for 44%, which ranks the first ==in library users.==

修改：To begin with, full-time **undergraduates** account for 44%, which ranks first ==among library users.==

改动：删去冗余 the percentage of；undergraduate → **undergraduates**；删除多余 the

原句：In contrast, academic staff and distance learning of all courses ==make up the less proporation==, at 7% and 8% respectively.

修改：In contrast, academic staff and **distance learning students** ==make up **the smallest proportions**==, at 7% and 8% respectively.

改动：distance learning of all courses → **distance learning students**（表达不通顺，还原含义）；less proporation → **smallest proportions**（修正比较级误用 + 拼写）

原句：Besides, the percentage of full-time postgraduate ==make up for a quarter,== while the proporation of part-time postgraduate constitute 16%.

修改：Besides, full-time **postgraduates** ==make up a quarter==, while part-time **postgraduates** constitute 16%.

改动：删除冗余 the percentage of；删掉错误介词**for**（make up for = 弥补）；postgraduate → **postgraduates**；修正拼写 proporation

## 表格主体段（最严重数据颠倒问题）

原句：Apart from this, all readers ==satisfied with== the helpfulness of staff and library opening hours, while 65% of them very satisfied with the former and 95% of them very satisfied with the latter.

修改：Apart from this, all readers ==**are satisfied** with== the helpfulness of staff and library opening hours. **95% of them are very satisfied with staff, while 65% are very satisfied with opening hours.**

改动：补充缺失 be 动词**are**；✅**修正致命数据颠倒错误**（former/latter 数据写反，Task Achievement 重大扣分点）；拆分长句，消除逻辑错误

原句：==A samll part== of ==readers present that== they are not satisfied with the availability of books, availability of journals and reliability of ==wi-fi==, at 10%, 20% and 19% respectively.

修改：==A **small** part== of ==readers **state** that== they are not satisfied with the availability of books, availability of journals and reliability of ==**Wi-Fi**==, at 10%, 20% and 19% respectively.

改动：samll → **small**（拼写）；present → **state**（用词不当）；wi-fi → **Wi-Fi**

原句：In addition, nearly half of users show that they are very ==satisfied with them==, at 50%, 45% and 48% respectively.

修改：In addition, nearly half of users report that they are very ==satisfied with **these three services**==, at 50%, 45% and 48% respectively.

改动：them → **these three services**（消除代词指代模糊）；show → report（图表更合适）

原句：The rest of the readers are farily satisfied with them.

修改：The rest of the readers are **fairly** satisfied with **these services**.

改动：farily → **fairly**（拼写）；them → **these services**，避免指代不清
