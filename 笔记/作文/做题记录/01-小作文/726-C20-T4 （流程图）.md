## 7.0 范文

The flow chart illustrates the process of manufacturing fabric from bamboo plants.
该流程图展示了以竹子生产织物的工序。

Overall, the production of bamboo fabric is a linear procedure consisting of nine distinct stages, from initial planting in spring to the final creation of clothing products. The process can be broadly divided into three phases: raw material preparation, fibre processing, and fabric production.
总体来看，竹纤维织物的生产是一道线性工序，一共分为九个不同阶段，从春季最初的种植直至最终制成衣物成品。整个流程大致可以划分为三个阶段：原料准备、纤维处理以及织物织造。

To begin with, bamboo plants are grown in spring and then harvested during autumn. The harvested bamboo is first cut into thin strips, after which these strips are crushed to produce liquid pulp.
首先，竹子在春季种植，到秋季进行收割。收割后的竹子先被切成细竹条，随后竹条被粉碎，制成液态纸浆。

In the next stage of the process, the liquid pulp is filtered to separate long fibres from the liquid. Water and amine oxide are then added to the fibres to soften them. Following this softening step, the treated fibres are spun into yarn, which is subsequently woven into finished fabric. Finally, the bamboo fabric is made into items of clothing such as socks and T-shirts.
流程下一阶段，液态浆体经过过滤，将长纤维与液体分离开。随后向纤维中加水与氧化胺进行软化处理。软化完成后，处理过的纤维被纺成纱线，纱线再织成成品布料。最后，竹织物被加工成袜子、T 恤等服饰。
