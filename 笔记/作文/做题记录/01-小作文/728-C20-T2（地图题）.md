## 🔴 时态致命错误【最高优先级】

地图题描述两个时间点对比：**1950（一般过去时）、如今（一般现在时）；变化使用 `was transformed / has been turned`**

全文大量滥用 `had been constructed/had been built`

> ❌ had been done = 过去的过去，本文没有第三个时间参照，属于严重语法错误！

## 🔴 事实 / 方位错误（Task Achievement 重大失分）

1. 河流走向描述错误：河流从西南蜿蜒至东南，不是东北向西南；
2. Sheep 场地在**农场北部**，不是东北；
3. 羊圈区域改造后分为露营地 + 太阳能板，原文方位描述混乱；
4. **遗漏 Holiday cottages（假日小屋）**，重要新增设施缺失；
5. `the sheep was` 主谓不一致；`separated to` 搭配错误（正确：separated into）

## 🔴 拼写 & 基础搭配错误

1. paralleoed → parallel
2. founctions → functions
3. `north-eastern of` 语法错误 → **the north-east of / the northeastern part of**
4. parking 可数概念：a parking area /two parking areas，不能直接写`two parkings`
5. `along with the two roads` 语义完全不通

## 🔴 逻辑事实错误

> such as parking, farm shop and barn
> 
> ❌ Barn 1950 年本来就存在，**不是新建建筑**！属于严重事实错误！

### 第 1 句

原文：These two maps illustrate the area of the Beechwood Farm in 1950 and how it looks today.

问题：area 用词欠佳；专有名词多余 the

修订：These two maps ==illustrate the site of Beechwood Farm== in 1950 and its current layout.

### 第 2、3 句（Overview 总结段）

原文：Overall, more buildings had been constructed across this period, such as parking, farm shop and barn. The sheep located in the north-eastern of the farm had been separated to several areas for different founctions.

致命错误：

① had been constructed 时态错误；② barn 1950 年已有，不是新建；③ parking 不可直接裸用；④ north-eastern of 语法错误；⑤ separated to 搭配错误；⑥ founctions 拼写错误

修订：Overall, ==a variety of new facilities have been built over this period==, ==including== parking areas and a farm shop. The sheep pasture ==in the northern part of== the farm ==has been divided into multiple areas with distinct functions.==

### 第 4、5 句

原文：There is a river, which comes from north-eastern corner to south-west and bends at the bottom of the map. In 1950, the tracks were in the north of the river, which were paralleoed to the river approximately. But they had been converted to roads by this period.

致命错误：河流走向描述错误；paralleoed 拼写错误；时态错误；重复 the river

修订：A river ==winds from the southwest== and ==curves along the southern boundary of the farm.== In 1950, tracks to the north of the river ==ran roughly parallel to the waterway==, and these paths have since been converted into roads.

### 第 6、7、8 句

原文：There was an area between the roads and river, which was separated to three areas, for planting fruit trees, soft fruits and vegetables respectively. A farm shop had been built, which was situated in the fruit trees' area, along the road. Turning to the eastern part, there were barn, farmhouse and chickens' area from north to south, but the barn had been relocated to the south of the triangle road.

致命错误：separated to；时态错误；名词缺少冠词；triangle road 表达生硬；遗漏假日小屋

There was an area between the roads and the river, which ==was separated **into** three areas== for fruit trees, soft fruits and vegetables respectively. A farm shop **has been built**, which is situated in the fruit trees area along the road. ==Turning to the eastern part==, there **was a** barn, farmhouse and **chicken** area ==from north to south==, but the barn ==**has been relocated** to== the south of the triangular road. **Holiday cottages have also been built beside the farmhouse.**

### 第 9、10 句

原文：The sheep was in the north of the road, which had been separated to camping field and solar panels. Two parkings had been constructed, the one was located in the east, along with the two roads, the other was situated in the south of solar panels.

致命错误：sheep was 主谓不一致；separated to；parkings 可数错误；逗号粘连句；along with 语义错误；方位描述模糊

修订：The sheep field ==was to the north of the road==, which ==**has been separated into**== a camping field and ==an area for solar panels==. Two parking areas **have been constructed**. ==One is located in the east near the two roads==, and the other ==is situated to the south of== the solar panels.
