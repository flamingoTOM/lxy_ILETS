### 致命错误

1. **时态乱用**：地图题描述“过去”用**一般过去时 (was/were)**，描述“现在”用**一般现在时 (is/are)** 或**现在完成时 (has/have been)**。文中滥用过去完成时 (had been) 不仅多余，而且语法错误（在没有过去参照时间点的情况下，不能用 had been）。
    
2. **介词致命伤**：
    
    - `in the south of` = 在内部（南边）。户外区域在外部，必须用 `to the south of` 或 `outside the southern side`。
        
    - `in the west of this area` = 在该区域内部西侧（勉强对，但更地道的方位词是 `on the western side`）。
        
    - `In the east of it` = 在它内部东边，改为 `To the east of it`（外部相邻）。
        
3. **词汇搭配错误**：`takes place`（发生/举行）≠ `occupies/takes up`（占据）。
    
4. **拼写与冠词**：`Toliets` → `Toilets`；`Turing` → `Turning`；`before cafe` → `before **the** cafe`。

> **原句 1**: The ==plans== illustrate a college cafe before it was redesigned and ==how it looks today.==  
> **修改 1**: **The ==provided plans== illustrate a college café before its redesign and ==its current layout**.==  
> _(理由：简化句式，避免笨拙的 before...and how... 结构，使用 its 指代更高级。)_

> **原句 2**: Overall, The space for tables and chairs ==takes place the biggest area== in the ==college cafe.==  
> **修改 2**: **Overall, the area designated for tables and chairs ==occupies the largest proportion of== the ==indoor space**.==  
> _(理由：修正致命搭配 takes place → occupies；修正 T 大写；用 designated for 丰富词汇；明确 indoor 与下文 outdoor 区分。)_

> **原句 3**: Obviously, after being redesigned, an outdoor seating area ==had been added== ==in the south of the cafe.==  
> **修改 3**: **Notably, an outdoor seating area ==has been added== ==to the south of the building**==.  
> _(理由：Obviously 口语化，改为 Notably；修正时态 had been → has been (表示对现在的影响)；修正介词 in → to (外部)。)_

> **原句 4**: In the northern part of the cafe, there were kitchen and serving area for all food and drink before cafe was redesigned.  
> **修改 4**: **In the northern part of the cafe, there was a kitchen and a serving area for all food and drink before the renovation**.  
> _(理由：there were 改 was (kitchen 单数)；补充冠词 a；before cafe was redesigned 换为 before the renovation 避免重复。)_

> **原句 5**: The serving area ==had been converted to== ==a place for hot meals.==  
> **修改 5**: **This serving area ==was converted into== ==a section for hot meals**.==  
> _(理由：删除不必要过去完成时，改为一般过去时 was converted；搭配 convert into；a place 换为 a section 避免低级词。)_

> **原句 6**: Turing to the main area, a salad bar ==had been constructed== ==in the west of this area.==  
> **修改 6**: **Turning to the main seating area, a salad bar ==was constructed== ==on its western side**.==  
> _(理由：修正拼写 Turing → Turning；删除 had been；in the west of this area 换为 on its western side (更地道且避免重复 area)。)_

> **原句 7**: Toliets were situated in the ==southern-east corner==, which ==stayed unchanged.==  
> **修改 7**: **The toilets, located in the ==south-eastern corner==, ==remained unchanged**.==  
> _(理由：修正拼写 Toliets；southern-east 改为 south-eastern；使用插入语结构 located... 提升句式多样性。)_

> **原句 8**: There was a bin next to the toilets, which ==had been enlarged== and ==converted to== recycling bins.  
> **修改 8**: **The bin next to them ==was enlarged== and ==converted into== recycling bins**.  
> _(理由：them 指代 toilets 避免重复；删除 had been；convert into；注意原单数 bin 变成复数回收箱，隐含扩建改造，表达合理。)_

> **原句 9**: In the northern area, there were two distinct staff dining rooms, which ==had been replaced by== a coffee bar and ==an area for take away food.==  
> **修改 9**: **In the northern area, the two separate staff dining rooms ==were replaced by== a coffee bar and ==a take-away food section**.==  
> _(理由：删除 had been；distinct 换为 separate 避免重复；简化 an area for take away food 为复合形容词 a take-away food section。)_

> **原句 10**: A barbecue was newly designed, which was located in the ==northern-west corner== outdoors.  
> **修改 10**: **A barbecue was newly added, positioned outdoors in the ==north-western corner**.==  
> _(理由：designed 改为 added 更符合“新增”题意；northern-west 改为 north-western；调整语序让 outdoors 位置更自然。)_

> **原句 11**: ==In the east of it,== a place ==was designed== for outdoor seating.  
> **修改 11**: ==**To the east of this barbecue,== an outdoor seating area ==was created**.==  
> _(理由：修正介词 In → To (外部)；it 指代不明，明确为 this barbecue；a place 换为 an outdoor seating area 与原文呼应，且避免与前句重复。)_
