The line chart gives information about the number of jobs in four sectors of the economy in the US over the 60-year period from 1960 to 2020.

Overall, the number of jobs in retail and healthcare witnessed a constant growth across the whole period, while the figure for jobs in agriculture droped continuously. The quantity of works in manufacturing ranked the highest in the first 40 years, but it was overtaken by retail and healthcare in the last 20 years of this period.

To begin with, the figure for works in manufacturing was 15 millions in 1960, which rised to a peak approximately 20 millions in 1980, before falling constantly to just about 13 millions by 2020. The quantity of jobs in retail and healthcare was roughly 6 millions and 2 millions respectively in 1960, both of which rised continuously to about 16 millions in 2020. In 1960, the number of occupations in agriculture was approximately 6 millions, but it droped constanly and slightly in the next 60 years, which was only 2 millions in 2020.

---

### 1. 词形 / 拼写硬伤（高频扣分）

- ❌ `droped` → ✅ `dropped`（过去式需双写末尾 p）
- ❌ `rised` → ✅ `rose`（rise 为不规则动词，过去式是 rose，不存在 rised，属于严重语法错误）
- ❌ `constanly` → ✅ `constantly`（拼写错误）
- ❌ `15 millions` → ✅ `15 million`（数字 + million/thousand 时，单位永远不加 s）

### 2. 用词搭配硬伤

- ❌ `works` 表 “工作岗位” 错误：work 表 “工作” 是不可数名词，works 多指 “作品、工厂”；描述岗位统一用 `jobs`
- ❌ `quantity of jobs` 搭配不当：quantity 多修饰不可数名词，描述可数岗位数量用 `number of jobs` 或 `the figure for`

### 3. 语法 / 指代硬伤

- ❌ `which was only 2 millions in 2020`：which 指代模糊，不能指代前文整句，需明确写 `the figure`
- ❌ 概述段主语逻辑错误：`the quantity of works... ranked the highest` 主语应为 “岗位数量”，而非 “工作量”，属于搭配 + 逻辑双重错误

### 4. 趋势描述偏差（TA 扣分）

- 农业并非 “持续小幅下降”：1960-1980 年快速下降，1980-2000 年基本平稳，2000 年后小幅下降，描述为 `constantly and slightly` 不符合图表事实

## 第一段（引言）

原句：The line chart gives information about the number of jobs in four sectors of the economy in the US over the 60-year period from 1960 to 2020.

修改：The line chart illustrates the number of jobs in four economic sectors in the US ==over the 60-year period from 1960 to 2020.==

说明：`illustrates` 是小作文图表标准用词，优于 gives information about。

## 第二段（Overview 概述段）

原句 1：Overall, the number of jobs in retail and healthcare witnessed a constant growth across the whole period, while the figure for jobs in agriculture dropped continuously.

修改：Overall, the number of jobs in retail and healthcare ==witnessed constant growth== across the period, while ==the figure for== agriculture ==fell continuously.==

说明：growth 表趋势不可数，删掉多余`a`；across the whole period 简化；重复的 jobs 可以省略。

原句 2：The number of occupations in manufacturing ranked the highest in the first 40 years, but it was overtaken by retail and healthcare in the last 20 years of this period.

修改：Manufacturing had the highest number of jobs in the first four decades, but ==it was overtaken by== retail and healthcare ==in the final 20 years.==

说明：occupations 略显生硬；the last 20 years of this period 冗余精简。

## 第三段（主体第一段）

原句 1：To begin with, the figure for jobs in manufacturing was 15 million in 1960, which rose to a peak approximately 20 million in 1980, before falling constantly to just about 13 million by 2020.

修改：To begin with, the figure for manufacturing jobs stood at 15 million in 1960. It ==rose to a peak of about== 20 million in 1980, ==before falling steadily to== just about 13 million by 2020.

致命错误修复：peak 后面缺少介词 of；constantly 描述数据趋势替换为小作文高频词 steadily；拆分长句更流畅。

原句 2：The figure for jobs in retail and healthcare was roughly 6 million and 2 million respectively in 1960, both of which grew continuously to about 16 million in 2020.

修改：The figures for retail and healthcare jobs ==stood at== roughly 6 million and 2 million respectively in 1960, and both ==grew continuously== to around 16 million by 2020.

说明：两个行业，figure 改为复数 figures；both of which 从句略显笨重，微调更自然。

## 第四段（主体第二段）

原句 1：In 1960, the number of occupations in agriculture was approximately 6 millions, it dropped to roughly 4 million in 1980.

修改：In 1960, the number of jobs in agriculture ==stood at== approximately 6 million. This figure dropped to roughly 4 million in 1980.

❗致命错误：原句逗号直接连接两个完整句子（comma splice，严重扣分）；数字后 million 不加 s。

原句 2：After that, it remained stable between 1980 and 2000, then declined slightly in the next 20 years, which was only 2 million in 2020.

修改：After that, it ==remained stable== between 1980 and 2000 ==before declining slightly== ==over the following two decades==, ==reaching== only 2 million in 2020.

❗致命错误：which 指代模糊；现在分词 reaching 精准表达最终数值，是小作文高分句式。
