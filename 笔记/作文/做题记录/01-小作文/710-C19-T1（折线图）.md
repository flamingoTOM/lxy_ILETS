# 3.细节
To begin with, participation in the film club remained the highest of all activities, fluctuating between approximately 60 and 66 over the two decades.
首先，电影俱乐部的参与人数始终是所有活动中最高的，在这二十年间于约 60 至 66 人之间波动。

Similarly, the number of people ==joining== martial arts classes ==stayed relatively stable==, varying between 32 and 38 ==across the whole period.==
与之类似，参加武术课程的人数保持相对稳定，在整个时间段内在 32 至 38 人之间浮动。

In contrast, participation in amateur dramatics ==rose slightly to a peak of about== 28 in 2005, before ==falling significantly to== just 6 participants by 2020.
相比之下，业余戏剧表演的参与人数在 2005 年小幅上升至约 28 人的峰值，随后到 2020 年大幅下降至仅 6 人。

Table tennis ==had a stable participation rate== of around 20 in the first decade, but after 2010 this figure rose sharply, ==reaching approximately== 54 in 2020.
乒乓球项目在头十年的参与人数稳定在 20 人左右，但 2010 年后该数值急剧上升，到 2020 年达到约 54 人。

Finally, musical performances ==had no participants== in 2000 and 2005, but the number grew steadily ==from 2010 onwards==, hitting roughly 18 ==by the end of the period.==
最后，音乐表演项目在 2000 年和 2005 年尚无参与者，但人数从 2010 年起稳步增长，到该时段结束时达到约 18 人。
