This flow chart illustrate the formation process of a rain-shadow desert.
It is noticeable that there are siven distinct stages in the formation process of a rain-shadow desert. These mainly include winds preparation and moist air rising, clouds formation and rain falling, dry air dropping and exiting.
At the first stage in the process, winds will approach coast from the sea, while they are pushed upwards along the windward side due to the barrier of the mountains. After that, with the temperature declining, the winds are converted to moist air. Clouds are formed subsequently after moist air rising and cooling. At stage five, rain falls on the top of the mountains. Following this step, dry air continues over mountain, dropping along the leeward side. Last but not least, dry winds hit inland areas and rain-shadow desert is finally formed. The whole process spans thousands of kilometres.

This flow chart **illustrates** the formation process of a rain-shadow desert.

**原句 2**

→ **修改后**  
It is noticeable that there are **seven** distinct stages in the formation process of a rain-shadow desert.

**原句 3**  
These mainly include ==winds preparation== and ==moist air rising==, clouds formation and rain falling, ==dry air dropping and exiting.==  
→ **修改后**  
These mainly include ==**the approach of winds,== ==the rising of moist air==, cloud formation, rainfall, and ==the descent of dry air**.==

**原句 4**  
At the first stage in the process, winds ==**will approach coast**== from the sea, **while** they are pushed upwards along the windward side due to the barrier of the mountains.  
→ **修改后**  
At the first stage in the process, winds ==**approach the coast**== from the sea, **and** they are pushed upwards along the windward side due to the barrier of the mountains.

**原句 5**  
After that, with the temperature declining, ==the winds **are converted to moist air**.==  
→ **修改后**  
After that, with the temperature declining, ==the **air becomes moist**.==

**原句 6**  
Clouds are formed subsequently ==**after moist air rising and cooling**.==  
→ **修改后**  
Clouds are formed subsequently ==after the moist air **rises and cools**.==

|结构|例句|规则|
|---|---|---|
|**after + 动名词 (doing)**|_After finishing homework, I went out._|动名词的**逻辑主语**必须和主句的**主语一致**（都是 I）|
|**after + 句子（主谓宾）**|_After the moist air **rises and cools**, clouds form._|从句有**自己的主语**（the moist air），跟主句主语（clouds）**不同**|

> **如果从句的主语和主句的主语不是同一个人/物，就不能用 after doing，必须用 after + 完整句子。**

**原句 8**

→ **修改后**  
Following this step, dry air continues over **the** mountain, ==**descending** along the leeward side.==

**原句 9**  
Last but not least, dry winds hit inland areas and ==**rain-shadow desert**== is finally formed.  
→ **修改后**  
Last but not least, dry winds hit inland areas and ==**a** rain-shadow desert== is finally formed.
