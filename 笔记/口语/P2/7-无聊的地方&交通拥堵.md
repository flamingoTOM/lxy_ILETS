Today, I'll talk about ==a trip to the Qinling Mountains== with my parents last year. The mountains are ==not far away,== ==right to the south of the city==. We planned to ==go hiking there== ==early in the morning==, but unfortunately, we ==ran into a huge traffic jam== for nearly two hours. 

今天我想讲讲去年夏天我和父母去秦岭的一次旅行。秦岭就在城市南边不远处。我们原本计划一大早去那里徒步，但不巧的是，我们遇上大堵车，被困了将近两个小时。


I felt really bored at that time. ==There was nothing to see but endless cars.== I tried to ==listen to some podcasts== about technology and AI. I hoped they could help me ==stay sane==, but it didn't work. I also ==chatted with my parents== and ==called my grandparents.==

When we arrived at ==the foot of the mountain==, I had hoped to ==take a break from urban life,== ==soak up some fresh air== and ==recharge my batteries.== But ==there was rubbish everywhere== ==along the road== ==up to the mountain.== I was really disappointed, so we decided to ==go back before reaching the top.==

当我们抵达山脚下时，我原本期盼远离城市的喧嚣、呼吸新鲜空气、休整放松。可是上山的沿途随处都是垃圾。于是我们决定还没登顶就折返下山。


==Even now==, I still think this trip was ==the most boring one== ==I have ever had==, and I would never ==recommend it to others.==
