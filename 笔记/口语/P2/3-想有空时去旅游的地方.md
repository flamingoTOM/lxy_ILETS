# Describe a place you would like to visit in your free time

You should say:

- Where it is
- What you will do there
- How long you will stay there
- And explain why you would like to visit it

==One place I’d like to visit in my free time== is Yangshuo. It’s located in Guilin, Guangxi Province ==in southern China.== ==There is a famous saying:== ==Guilin’s scenery ranks first under heaven,== and ==Yangshuo’s scenery tops Guilin.==

闲暇时我想去游览的一个地方是阳朔。它位于中国南部广西桂林市。有一句广为流传的说法：桂林山水甲天下，阳朔山水甲桂林。


I first visited Yangshuo ==from an early age, approximately when I was in primary school,== ==together with my parents.== To be honest, ==it did not leave a deep impression on me== back then. I only remembered that ==the weather was hot and humid,== and ==there were countless mountains.==
我小时候第一次去阳朔，大概是上小学的时候，和父母一同前往。说实话，那时阳朔并没有给我留下深刻印象。我只记得当地天气闷热潮湿，群山连绵不绝。


==In recent years,== ==I have got into rock climbing==. After ==practising for two months,== I really want to ==try outdoor climbing==. After watching lots of ==climbing vlogs== on YouTube and Bilibili,I ==found out== that Yangshuo is ==the birthplace of outdoor rock climbing in China==. The temperature there ==stays around 20 degrees all year round==. ==The most impressive feature== is ==its unique karst landscape==, and ==there are more than 70 climbing crags.==

近几年，我爱上了攀岩。经过两个月练习后，我十分想要尝试野外攀岩。我在油管和 B 站观看了许多攀岩短视频后了解到，阳朔是中国野外攀岩的发源地。当地全年气温大约维持在 20 摄氏度。最吸引人的就是独特的喀斯特地貌，而且这里拥有 70 余处攀岩岩壁。


==If I get the opportunity,== I plan to ==travel there and climb for several months.== I hope to ==take a break from urban life,== ==soak up some fresh air== and ==recharge my batteries.== ==That’s exactly why I hope to travel there.==

如果有机会，我打算前往阳朔，在那里攀岩数月。我希望暂时远离城市生活，呼吸新鲜空气，好好休整恢复状态。这正是我想去那里旅行的原因。
