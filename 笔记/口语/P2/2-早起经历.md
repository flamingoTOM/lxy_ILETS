To be honest, I’m not really a morning person. Normally, ==I feel more energetic and focused== in the afternoon, so I usually ==deal with difficult preoblems== at that time. But ==things totally changed== last year ==when I got into rock climbing.==

说实话，我算不上习惯早起的人。通常我下午精力更充沛、注意力更集中，所以一般把难的任务放在下午处理。但去年我迷上攀岩之后，情况就完全变了。


==It was last September==. I went to Yangshuo, Guangxi Province ==in southern China==. Since I was still a student and didn’t have much money, I ==worked as a volunteer== ==at a local inn== ==for roughly two months.== ==The deal was great== — I worked one day and ==went rock climbing the next.==

那是去年九月，我去了中国南方广西的阳朔。因为当时还是学生，没什么钱，我就在当地一家客栈做了大约两个月义工。安排特别划算 —— 做一天工，第二天就去攀岩。


There I met ==my climbing partner== Xiaoya, who was also a volunteer. ==We got up at half past five== every morning and ==climbed TV Tower Mountain==. It took us about 30 minutes ==to reach the top==, ==just in time for the sunrise==. Of course, ==we couldn’t see it every day==. it depended on our luck

在那儿我认识了攀岩搭档小娅，她也是义工。我们每天五点半起床，去爬电视塔山，大概 30 分钟到山顶，刚好赶上日出。当然也不是天天都能看到，得碰上好天气才行。


==Getting up early== let me ==take a break from city life==, ==soak up fresh air== and ==fully recharge my batteries==. What’s more, there were two lovely dogs on the mountain. ==I brought two sausages for them== every morning, and they would ==walk all the way down with me==. That experience really ==left a deep impression on me.==

早起让我从城市生活里抽离出来，呼吸新鲜空气，好好调整状态。而且山上有两只很可爱的小狗，我每天早上带两根香肠喂它们，它们就会一路陪我下山。那段经历真的给我留下了很深的印象。


Overall, even though ==I’m still not a morning person==, that trip ==made me realise== early mornings ==can be really nice==. It was a very ==memorable experience.==

总的来说，虽然我现在还是不爱早起，但那次旅行让我发现清晨其实特别美好，是一段很难忘的经历。
