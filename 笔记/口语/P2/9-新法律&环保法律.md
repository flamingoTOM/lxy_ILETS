Today, I'll talk about a new ==environmental law== that ==should be introduced in CHINA. It is about ==stopping rubbish pollution== in ==outdoor space==. This law will ask everyone to ==take their own garbage away== ==after climbing mountains.== People who ==leave rubbish behind== will ==get a small fine.==

今天我想讲讲我国应该推行的一项新环境法。它是关于制止户外风景区垃圾污染的。这项法规要求所有游客登山后带走自己产生的垃圾。随意丢弃垃圾的人将会处以小额罚款。



This law will make our ==outdoor space== much cleaner. It can also protect ==wild animals==. I think most people will support this law; only ==a tiny fraction of people== think ==it is troublesome.==

这条规定能让自然景区干净很多，同时也能保护野生动物，我认为大多数人都会支持这项法规，只有少数人会觉得麻烦。



I ==came up with this idea== from ==a trip to the Qinling Mountains== with my parents last summer. We planned to ==go hiking== there, but when we arrived at ==the foot of the mountain,== there was ==rubbish everywhere== ==along the road up to the mountain==. I was really disappointed., so We decided to ==go back before reaching the top==. 

我产生这个想法源于去年夏天和父母去秦岭的一次旅行。我们原本打算去徒步，可当我们到达山脚下时，上山沿途到处都是垃圾。我们甚至没有登顶就决定折返，当时我特别失望。



Personally, I support this law. because I believe if this law ==**were introduced** in my country,== those beautiful mountains ==**would become cleaner**.== I would never recommend that place to others, but I would definitely recommend this law to everyone.

就我个人而言，我支持这项法规。我认为如果我国推行这条法规，那些美丽的景区将会变得更加干净。我绝不会向别人推荐那个地方，但我一定会向所有人推荐这条法规。
