Today, I'll talk about a movie called Free Solo, and I watched it at home with my dad last year. It really left a deep impression on me.

今天我想聊一部名为《徒手攀岩》的电影。去年我和爸爸在家观看了这部影片。它给我留下了十分深刻的印象

---

The movie is about ==a professional rock climber== named Alex Honnold. It records his ==entire process== of ==free solo climbing El Capitan==, which is a ==granite cliff== in ==Yosemite National Park== in the United States. Free solo means climbing ==without any ropes or safety equipment==, ==even a tiny mistake can cost you your life.==

这部电影讲述了职业攀岩者亚历克斯・霍诺尔德的故事。影片记录了他徒手攀登酋长岩的全过程，酋长岩是美国优胜美地国家公园内一座高达 3000 英尺的花岗岩峭壁。徒手攀岩就是不携带绳索、没有任何安全保护装备进行攀登，哪怕一个微小失误，都可能失去生命。

---

After that, I got into rock climbing. ==After climbing for two months==, I wanted to ==try outdoor climbing==. I found out that ==Yangshuo is the birthplace of outdoor rock climbing== in China. So I went there last September. Since I was still a student and didn’t have much money, I worked as a volunteer in a local inn for roughly two months. ==The deal was great== — I worked one day and went rock climbing the next.

看完电影之后，我开始迷上攀岩。练习攀岩两个月后，我想要尝试野外攀岩。我了解到阳朔是中国野外攀岩的发源地，于是去年九月我去到了那里。当时我还是学生，手头并不宽裕，就在当地一家客栈做了大约两个月的义工。这个安排很不错：工作一天，第二天就可以去攀岩。

---

I strongly recommend Free Solo to everyone, ==whether you are into rock climbing or not.==

向所有人强烈推荐《徒手攀岩》这部影片，无论你是否喜欢攀岩。
