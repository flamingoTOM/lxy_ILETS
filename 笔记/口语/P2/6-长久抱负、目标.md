Today I’d like to talk about a long-term goal I have. My goal is to ==become an engineer== in the field of autonomous driving. 

今天我想聊一个我的长期目标。我的目标是成为一名自动驾驶领域的工程师。

---
 I’ve had this goal for about **five years**, ever since I started my ==undergraduate studies== in ==Measurement, Control Technology and Instrument.==  I really enjoy my studies, especially those ==hands-on experiments==. Although some ==professional courses are challenging==, I can learn lots of ==practical skills==. It always ==makes me feel really rewarding.==

 Well, first, I’ll ==**finish my master’s degree**== in the next three years. ==At the same time==, I plan to ==**take some high-quality internships**== to ==**improve my practical skills**.== After graduation, I’ll ==**work as an engineer**== in an autonomous driving company.

I ==set this goal== for two reasons.

**为什么设立这个目标** – 我设立这个目标有两个原因。第一，我从小就对电子设备着迷，所以工程学是我的热情所在。第二，我希望**用专业知识解决现实问题**。具体来说，我想优化自动驾驶算法，让它既灵活又安全。
