### 1. 开题
I’d like to recommend Yangshuo to others. It’s located in Guilin, Guangxi Province ==in southern China==. ==There is a famous saying:== ==Guilin’s scenery ranks first under heaven,== and ==Yangshuo’s scenery tops Guilin.==

我想向大家推荐阳朔。它位于中国南部广西桂林。有一句著名的俗语：桂林山水甲天下，阳朔山水甲桂林。

### 2. 历史
I first visited Yangshuo ==from an early age, approximately when I was in primary school,== ==together with my parents.== To be honest, ==it did not leave a deep impression on me== back then. I only remembered that ==the weather was hot and humid==, and there were ==countless mountains.==

我小时候第一次来到阳朔，大概在上小学的时候，和父母一同前往。说实话，当时这里并没有给我留下很深的印象，我只记得气候湿热，随处都是山峰。

### 3. 过程
#### 起因
Last year, I ==got into rock climbing.== After ==climbing for two months==, I wanted to ==try outdoor climbing==. Then I ==found out== that Yangshuo is ==the birthplace of outdoor rock climbing== in China. The temperature there ==stays around 20 degrees== ==all year round==. and the ==unique karst landscape== is fantastic . and there are ==more than 70 climbing crags.==

去年，我迷上了攀岩。在室内攀岩馆练习两个月后，我想要尝试野外攀岩。随后我了解到，阳朔是中国野外攀岩的发源地。当地全年气温维持在 20 摄氏度左右。最让人难忘的特色就是独特的喀斯特地貌，这里拥有超过 70 处攀岩岩壁。

#### 事件
So I went there last September. ==Since I am still a student== and didn’t have much money, so I ==worked as a volunteer== in a ==local inn== for roughly two months. ==The deal was great== — I ==worked one day== and ==went rock climbing the next.== In the morning, I usually ==rode an electric bike== to the crags. It was really convenient. In the afternoon, I often ==had a bowl of rice noodles==, ==which only cost six yuan==. This period of time ==left a deep impression on me.==

于是去年九月我去到阳朔。因为我还是学生，没有太多积蓄。我在当地一家民宿做志愿者，持续大约两个月。我工作一天，隔天就去攀岩。我经常骑电动车前往攀岩点，出行十分方便。中午我常会吃一碗米粉，只需要 6 元。这段时光给我留下了极其深刻的印象。

### 4. 结果
Therefore, I strongly recommend Yangshuo to everyone, ==whether you are into rock climbing or not.==
