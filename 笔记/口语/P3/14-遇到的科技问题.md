## 1. What are the advantages and disadvantages of AI?

Advantages first. AI saves us lots of time. It can finish hard work quickly, like calculating data.
But it also has downsides. Some people depend on it too much. They lose their own thinking skills. Also, some jobs may disappear because of AI.

首先说优点。人工智能能帮我们节省大量时间。它可以快速完成复杂工作，比如计算数据。
但它也有弊端。有些人过度依赖人工智能，丧失独立思考能力。而且部分工作岗位会因为人工智能消失。

## 2. Do you think people today should learn about AI technology?

Yes, I believe everyone needs to know basic AI.

我认为所有人都应该了解基础人工智能知识。

Now AI is everywhere in daily life. If you understand it, you can use it better.

如今人工智能遍布日常生活。懂它，你才能更好地使用。

If you ignore it, you will fall behind others in study and work.

如果完全不了解，学习和工作上都会落后于人。

## 3. Should children learn to use AI?

Yes, but they need strict rules.

可以，但必须有严格使用规范。

AI helps kids search study information fast. It makes learning fun.

人工智能能帮孩子快速查找学习资料，让学习变得有趣。

However, kids cannot use it all the time. They may copy answers and stop thinking by themselves.

但孩子不能一直使用，他们会直接抄答案，不再独立思考。

## 4. How can AI help in our lives?

AI helps us in many daily areas.

人工智能在生活很多方面都能帮到我们。

First, it helps students finish homework and look up knowledge.

第一，帮助学生写作业、查阅知识。

Second, smart AI tools make housework easier, like robot cleaners.

第二，智能 AI 工具简化家务，比如扫地机器人。

Third, self-driving AI makes travelling safer and more convenient.

第三，自动驾驶 AI 让出行更安全、便捷。

## 5. Do you think students are overly reliant on AI?

Yes, lots of students rely on AI too much these days.

没错，现在很多学生过度依赖人工智能。

When they meet difficult problems, they open AI tools at once.

一遇到难题，他们立刻打开 AI 工具。

They do not try to think or calculate on their own. Slowly, their study skills become worse.

他们不会自己动脑思考、计算，久而久之，学习能力会变差。

## 6. What can teachers do to stop students from relying too much on AI?

Teachers can take three simple steps.

老师可以采取三个简单办法。

First, ask students to write down their own ideas before using AI.

第一，要求学生先用自己的思路解题，再使用 AI。

Second, give more in-class exams where phones and AI tools are banned.

第二，多安排课堂测验，禁止携带手机和 AI 工具。

Third, teach students the right way to use AI, only as a helper, not a replacement.

第三，教会学生正确使用 AI，把它当作辅助工具，而不是替代品。
