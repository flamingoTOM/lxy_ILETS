## 1.**Did you like going to parks as a child?**

Definitely, ==Hitting the park== was ==a highlight of my week== ==when I was a kid.== I went to parks every weekend to play football when I was in my primary school. I even thought of going professional. ==But unfortunately==, I didn’t succeed. ==Looking back==, I really enjoyed being a kid.

小时候去公园是我每周最期待的事。上小学时，我每个周末都去那儿踢足球，甚至梦想成为一名职业球员。可惜最后没能如愿。回想起来，我十分享受那段童年时光。

## 2. Do you still like going to parks now?

Absolutely, park is a place where I can relax ==after a long week==. I usually like to ==walk close to the water, close to the river, the lake, or the forest areas==, ==that is close to nature==. It lets me ==**take a break from urban life**==, ==**soak up some fresh air**== and ==fully **recharge my batteries**.== 

当然，公园是我熬过漫长一周后放松身心的地方。我常常喜欢在水边散步，河畔、湖边或是林间都可以，也就是贴近大自然的环境。在公园里，我能够暂时远离都市生活，呼吸新鲜空气，充分恢复精力。

## 3.Would you like to see more parks in your city?

**==Absolutely I would==**. Right now most of my ==spare time== ==is spent on my phone==. More parks would **give me ==a great place to** relax== ==after studying and working,==  I can ==**breathe some fresh air**== and ==**recharge my batteries== in the park. ==It’s definitely better than== ==staying at home all day.==

我当然希望（建设更多公园）。目前我的大部分闲暇时间都耗在手机上。更多公园能为我提供学习、工作之余放松的好去处。我可以在公园里呼吸新鲜空气、恢复精力，这肯定比整天待在家里要好得多。

## 4. Are there any parks you want to go to in the future?

Yes, for sure, ==I’ve always wanted to== visit ==Yosemite National Park== in the United States. I want see ==El Capitan==, ==a huge granite cliff==. It’s famous because Alex Honnold completed a free solo climb there. I’m ==a big fan of rock climbing==, so I really hope to have a look.

当然，我一直想去美国的优胜美地国家公园。我很想去看一看酋长岩这块巨大的花岗岩岩壁。它非常出名，攀岩运动员 Alex Honnold 曾在这里完成徒手攀岩，我很喜欢攀岩，所以我非常想去看一看
