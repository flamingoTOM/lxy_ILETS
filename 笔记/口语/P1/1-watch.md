## 1. Do you wear a watch?

==Well, to be honest==, I wear a watch ==all the time==. and ==I basically can’t live without it==. ==Its main function for me== is tracking my physical health data. Whenever I go running, hit the gym or climb mountains on weekends, it records my heart rate, pace and other exercise data.  ==It’s super practical==

没错，说真的，我每时每刻都会戴手表，我几乎离不开它。对我来说它最主要的用处是监测身体健康数据。每当我跑步、健身，或是周末去爬山时，它都会记录我的心率、配速还有其他运动数据。它特别实用。

## 2. Have you ever got a watch as a gift?

Yeah, I actually received a smartwatch ==as a birthday gift== from my parents last year. They knew I love sports, so they picked this one ==specially for== tracking my exercise data. ==It’s absolutely the most practical== present I’ve ever gotten.

有的，去年生日我爸妈送了我一块智能手表当礼物。他们知道我喜欢运动，所以特意选了这款可以记录运动数据的手表。这绝对是我收到过最实用的礼物。

## 3. Why do some people wear expensive watches?

Well, ==I think there are two main reasons for this==. First of all，==quite a few people== treat expensive watches ==as a symbol of social status==.They believe expensive watches can show their wealth and taste 
Besides, some people  just love the ==delicate craft== of mechanical watches and ==collect them as hobbies==. 
我认为主要有两点原因。首先，不少人将名贵手表视作社会地位的象征。他们觉得出席商务会谈或社交活动时，昂贵腕表能彰显自身财力与品味。
除此之外，还有一部分人单纯喜爱机械表精巧的工艺，并把收藏手表当作爱好。


## 4. Do you think it is important to wear a watch? Why?

Yes,abusolutely. ==I have to admit==, wearing a watch can really ==level up== your style and ==make you look more professional==. It’s also a nice way to ==show off== your personality without saying a word.

现在我一般都是直接看手机时间，确实更方便。不过不得不说，戴手表真的能提升你的气质，让你看起来更专业。而且，手表也是个很好的方式，能展示你的个性，不用多说一句话。
