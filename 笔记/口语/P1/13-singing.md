#### 1. Do you like singing? Why?

Not really. To be honest, I don’t like singing very much. I’m really bad at it and I often go out of tune. actually, I know nothing about music, Whenever I sing, I sound really bad.

算不上喜欢。说实话，我并不怎么爱唱歌。我唱得特别差，还总跑调。其实我对音乐一窍不通，每次开口唱都难听极了。

---

#### 2. Have you ever learnt how to sing?


No, I’ve never learned to sing properly. I never took any singing lessons, and nobody taught me at home. I just sing casually with friends, and I’ve never tried to get better at it.

没有，我从来没正经学过唱歌。我没上过任何歌唱课，家里也没人教过我。我只在和朋友相处时随便唱唱，也从来没想过要提升唱功。

---

#### 3. Who do you want to sing for?

Hmm, ==I’ve never really thought about it==. I guess I’d sing for my close friends and family, ==just for fun at parties.== I would never sing in front of a lot of people, because ==I’d feel too nervous and embarrassed.==

嗯，我还真没仔细想过这个问题。我觉得我会唱给好朋友和家人听，就是在聚会上图个乐。我绝不会在很多人面前唱歌，因为我会特别紧张、难为情。

---

#### 4. Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?

Yes, I definitely think so. Singing ==is an easy way for people to feel happy==. It can help them ==let out stress== and ==forget about daily worries==. What’s more, singing with friends is a great way to ==have fun== and ==get along better.==

当然了，我完全这么觉得。唱歌是人们获得快乐的简单方式。它能帮人们释放压力，忘掉日常的烦心事。而且和朋友一起唱歌既能玩得开心，还能增进彼此的感情。
