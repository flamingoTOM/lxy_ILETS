### 1. When did you start using social media?

Honestly, I started using social media pretty early, around primary school, when my friends were talking about WeChat and QQ. I was super curious and wanted to keep up with them, so I signed up the moment I got my first smartphone. Since then, social media has become a staple in my daily routine.

说实话，我接触社交媒体挺早的，大概在上小学那会儿，当时身边所有人都在聊微信和 QQ。我特别好奇，也想跟上朋友们的节奏，所以我拿到第一部智能手机就注册了账号。从那以后，社交媒体就成了我日常生活里必不可少的一部分。

### 2. Do you think you spend too much time on social media?

Well, sort of. I usually spend about one to two hours a day scrolling through short videos and checking messages. Sometimes it distracts me from my study when I can’t help picking up my phone. But I’m trying to set time limits to control it these days.

嗯，有点吧。我每天大概花一到两个小时刷短视频、查看消息。有时候忍不住拿起手机，就会耽误学习。不过最近我在试着设置时长限制来管控一下。

### 3. Do your friends use social media?

Yes, almost all of my friends use social media every day. We mainly use WeChat to keep in touch and share funny things in group chats. They also post their daily life or trip photos on Moments, and we often like and comment on each other’s posts.

是的，几乎我所有朋友每天都用社交媒体。我们主要用微信保持联系，在群聊里分享有意思的事。他们也会在朋友圈发日常生活或者旅行的照片，我们经常互相点赞评论。

### 4. What do people often do on social media?

Well, people do different things. Most use it to chat with friends and family. Many also scroll through short videos or read news for fun. Besides, some people share their daily moments, and others even use it for work or online learning.

嗯，人们做的事各不相同。大多数人用它和亲友聊天，很多人也会刷短视频或者看新闻消遣。除此之外，有些人会分享日常动态，还有人甚至用它办公或者线上学习。

### 5. Have you ever posted anything on social media?

Yes, I have, but not very frequently. I sometimes share photos of my trips, delicious food or small daily moments on WeChat Moments. Generally speaking, I prefer to browse other people’s posts rather than post a lot myself.

发过，但不是很频繁。我偶尔会在朋友圈分享旅行、美食的照片，或是生活里的小日常。总的来说，比起发很多动态，我更喜欢浏览别人的内容。
