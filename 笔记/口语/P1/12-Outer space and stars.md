# 3. Do you want to know more about outer space?

Definitely. I think outer space is ==full of mysteries==, and learning about it is really interesting.  I always watch some videos about planets online to ==satisfy my curiosity.== I’d love to ==find out more about== the universe later on.

当然想。我觉得外太空充满未解之谜，了解相关知识十分有趣。最近我偶尔会在网上看行星相关短视频，满足自己的好奇心。我以后也想更多地探索宇宙的奥秘。

# 4. Do you want to go into outer space in the future?

It has always been my life goal. Seeing the Earth from space ==would be really fantastic==. I even want to visit Mars ==just like people do in science movies.==
But space travel is far too expensive for ordinary people right now. Maybe I will give it a try when it becomes cheaper in the future.

这一直是我的人生目标。从太空俯瞰地球一定是无比震撼的体验，我甚至想像电影里那样去火星看看。但目前太空旅行对普通人来说实在太贵了。或许等到未来价格降低，我会去试一试。
