## 1. Do you use headphones?

==Well, to be honest==, I wear headphones nearly ==all the time==. and ==I basically can’t live without them==. ==Its main function for me== is to help me ==stay focused== when I’m studying or hitting the gym.

说实话，我几乎时时刻刻都戴着耳机，基本离不开它。对我来说，耳机最主要的作用就是在学习、跑步或者健身的时候帮我集中注意力。

## 2. What type of headphones do you use?

I've tried all kinds of headphones. To be honest, I prefer to use ==wireless headphones==. They are really convenient because ==there are no messy cables== to ==get in my way.==

我使用无线耳机。它们十分方便，不会有杂乱的线材造成困扰。

## 3. When would you use headphones?

I always wear headphones while running. I listen to some ==energetic music==, ==which can cheer me up== and help me ==keep going==, especially when I feel tired and want to ==give up halfway==.

跑步的时候我也会戴上耳机听歌。音乐能鼓舞我，让我坚持下去，尤其是当我疲惫想要半途而废的时候。

## 4. In what conditions would you not use headphones?

I will not wear headphones ==when staying with family or friends==, ==since it would be really impolite==. Besides, I avoid wearing them when ==crossing roads== or cycling ==for safety reasons.==

和家人朋友相处时我不会戴耳机，那样很不礼貌。除此之外，过马路或者骑车时，出于安全考虑我也不会佩戴。

## 5. Is wearing headphones comfortable？

Honestly, ==it is not that comfortable for me==. But I almost wear them everyday that ==I’ve already got used to it.==

说实话佩戴起来不算舒服。不过我经常使用，早就已经习惯了。
