## 1. Do you like science?

Yes, ==I’ve always been fascinated by== science and engineering ==from an early age==. I still remember ==a quote from== Doctor von Kármán: ==Scientists study the world as it is==; ==engineers create the world that has never been==. ==This idea really inspires me.==


喜欢，我从小就对科学与工程很感兴趣。我记得冯・卡门有一句话：科学家研究现存的世界，工程师创造从未有过的世界。这个观点一直激励着我。

## 2. When did you start to learn about science?

I first ==got to know== basic scientific knowledge in primary school science classes. Those ==hands-on experiments== and ==natural phenomena== made me more curious about how the world works.

我小学科学课第一次接触基础科学知识。那些简单实验与自然现象，激发了我对世界运行规律的好奇心。

## 3. Which science subject is interesting to you?

It's no doubt that Physics ==appeals to me the most==, especially mechanics. It explains the ==fundamental rules== behind motion, which ==connects closely== with engineering.

物理最吸引我，尤其是力学。它阐释运动背后的基础原理，和工程领域紧密相关。

## 4. What kinds of interesting things have you done with science?

I often watch science videos about ==mechanical structures==. Besides, I enjoy analysing ==how different devices operate== and try to understand ==the physical principles supporting their functions.==

我经常观看机械结构相关科普视频。除此之外，我喜欢分析各类设备的运作方式，弄懂支撑它们运转的物理原理。

## 5. Do you like watching science TV programs?

Sure, I’m ==keen on== science documentaries. They ==use smart ways to do explain difficult knowledge clearly== and ==constantly satisfy my curiosity about== technology and nature.

当然，我很喜欢科学纪录片。它把复杂理论通俗地展现出来，不断满足我对于科技和自然的好奇心。

## 6. Do Chinese people often visit science museums?

Science museums are very popular, especially for ==families with kids== and ==student school trips==. I ==used to love visiting them== when I was a kid, but these days ==I’m not really interested anymore.==

科技馆人气相当高，很多带小孩的家庭和学生研学团都会去。我小时候特别喜欢去，但现在已经没什么兴趣了。
