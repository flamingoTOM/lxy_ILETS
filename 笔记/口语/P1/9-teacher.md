###  1.Do you have a favorite teacher?

Absolutely. My favourite teacher was my high school physics teacher. He was really humorous and witty. He always used smart ways to explain the most difficult knowledge clearly. So I actually looked forward to his lessons, and he really left a deep impression on me.

当然。我最喜欢的老师是我高中物理老师。他十分风趣幽默，总能用巧妙的方式把最难的知识点讲明白。所以我那时候特别期待上他的课，他给我留下了非常深刻的印象。

### 2. In what way has your favourite teacher helped you?

Well, he helped me in many ways. Most importantly, he used smart ways to explain difficult knowledge clearly, which made me become interested in physics and improved my grades a lot. Besides, he always encouraged me when I failed exams or felt stressed, I'm really thankful to him.

嗯，他在很多方面都帮到了我。最重要的是，他能用巧妙的方式把难懂的知识讲明白，这让我对物理产生了兴趣，成绩也提升了很多。除此之外，当我考试失利或者感到压力大的时候，他总会鼓励我，我很感谢他


### 3. Are you still in touch with your primary school teachers?

Actually, not really. I’ve lost touch with most of my primary school teachers since I graduated. because We don’t have each other’s contact information
. But I still remember some of them clearly, as they were really kind to me.

其实没有了。小学毕业之后，我就和大部分小学老师失去了联系。我们现在没有彼此的联系方式，
但我依然清楚地记得其中一些老师，他们当时对我特别好。

---

### 4. Do you have a teacher from your past that you still remember?

Yes, definitely. My high school physics teacher is the one I remember the most. He was humorous and witty, and he always made boring lessons fun. He left such a deep impression on me that I can still recall some details of his classes now.

当然有。我印象最深的就是我的高中物理老师。他幽默风趣，总能把枯燥的课堂变得有意思。他给我留下了特别深刻的印象，到现在我都还能想起他课堂上的一些细节。

---

### 5. Do you want to be a teacher in the future?

To be honest, I don’t really want to be a teacher. I think teaching is a challenging job that requires a lot of patience and energy. I’m more interested in engineering work, which is also closer to my university major and my career plan.

说实话，我不太想当老师。我觉得教师是一份很有挑战的工作，需要投入大量的耐心和精力。我对工程类工作更感兴趣，这也和我的大学专业以及职业规划更贴合。

---

### 6. Do you like your primary school teachers more than your high school teachers?

Hmm, it’s hard to say which I like more. My primary school teachers were very warm and took good care of us in daily life. But my high school teachers taught me more useful learning skills and helped me prepare for university. I respect both of them for different reasons.

嗯，很难说我更喜欢哪一类。小学老师很亲切，在生活上把我们照顾得很好。但高中老师教给了我更多实用的学习方法，也帮我为大学做好了准备。出于不同的原因，这两类老师我都很尊敬。
