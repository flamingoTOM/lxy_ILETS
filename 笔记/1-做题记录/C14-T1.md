27.被错误选项干扰！对题干词汇不熟悉

15.同义词转换不敏感
原文：Nevertheless, the council unanimously ==rejected== the plan.

对应匹配：an explanation of why a proposed bike-sharing scheme was ==turned down==

16.读到了关键句，但忽略了关键，注意力跑到了专利上
原文：But financially I ==didn't really benefit from it==, because I never filed for a patent.
但我并没有真正从中获得利润,因为我从未申请过专利。
对应匹配：a reference to a person being ==unable to profit from== their work
提及某人无法从自身工作中获利
