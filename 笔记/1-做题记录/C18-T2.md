21.没听懂，没抓住关键词
as well as having ==massive== social and economic consequences.
以及产生了巨大的社会和经济后果。
对应
 It had a ==profound== effect on society.

23.没听懂，没抓住关键词
题干没读懂
contemporary sources → 同期史料 / 一手原始记载
contemporary：同期的、当时的
sources：史料、文献、原始记载
Michelle: Reports from the period blamed the haze for an ==increase in headaches, respiratory issues and asthma attacks.== 
当时的报告将这种雾气归咎于引发头痛、呼吸问题和哮喘发作的增加。
对应题干
it was associated with ==health issues==

24.没听懂，没抓住关键词
Michelle: It was Benjamin Franklin who ==realised that before anyone else.==
是本杰明·富兰克林比其他人先意识到这一点。
对应题干：
was the first to identify the reason for the haze.

25-26 以为是两段读音，压根没认真听

| 原文                                       | 题干                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| the effects would ==go on for so long.== | how ==long-lasting== the effects were |
| There's quite a ==varied range.==        | how widespread the effects were       |

29.匹配题，没听懂，没有抓住关键
According to one report it was about double the usual number and included an unusually high percentage of ==people under the age of 25.==
根据一份报告，死亡人数是平时的两倍，其中年龄不到25岁的人比例异常高。
对应
There was a significant increase in deaths of ==young people.==

37.对 waist 单词不熟悉
Women tied the pockets around their ==waist== so that they hung beneath their clothes.
女性将口袋系在腰间，这样它们就悬挂在衣服下面。
对应题干：
Pockets hung from the women's ==waist== under skirts and petticoats.

negative effect on the ==image== of women.
