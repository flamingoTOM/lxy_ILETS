28.题干不熟，短语不懂
A.**Its appeal lay in the radical nature of its ideas.**   它的吸引力来源于其观点的激进性。

- lay in = 在于
- radical nature：激进的特质
C. **It presented a view that was in line with the attitudes of its time.**
该书提出的观点与它所处时代的主流观念相一致。
- be in line with：与…… 契合、相一致（雅思高频同义短语）
- attitudes of its time：时代风气、当时主流社会观念
原文：
became popular because it ==fitted so well with== - and helped to justify - the competitive and individualistic ethos that was so ==prevalent== ==in late 20th-century societies.==
